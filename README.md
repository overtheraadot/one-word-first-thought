One word, first thought: **blanket**.

No topic, no explanation, no second draft — just the first word that comes to mind. Mine was "blanket": the soft thing you reach for before the day starts, the warm weight that makes a cold morning bearable, and the oldest hiding place in the world — pull it over your head and the whole day has to wait. Comfort, warmth, and a small act of retreat, all in seven letters.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes exactly one word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (`/output/entry.txt` in the grader sandbox): `blanket`.
